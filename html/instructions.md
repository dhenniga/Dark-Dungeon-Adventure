
# Dark Dungeons Instrustions

Dark Dungeons is a 2D dungeon exploration game where the player is tasked to survive the
various creatures and pitfall and reach the end before sucumming to the dank stench of 
the dungeon.  You'll need to find keys to unlock doors, 

### Player

The player can move in **four directions** but can only fight horizontally.

Press "X" to swing your sword or if the interaction icon is visible above the players
head, you can read signs, collect keys, push buttons, open doors and chests.

The player starts with five hearts.  Hearts are dedcuted if a player is attacked by an enemy, stands on a spike or falls down a hole.  Once all five hearts are gone, it's game over.

### Keys

Keys open doors.

Doors need keys to be opened.
